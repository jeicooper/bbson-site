About the Company

BBSON Technology, Inc. was incorporated on June 7, 2005 as a domestic corporation registered with the Philippine Economic Zone Authority (PEZA) as a zone export enterprise under Presidential Decree No. 66.

The company is authorized to engage in the manufacturing, assembling, importing, buying, and selling of goods related to metal and plastic spray painting, and electronic components — including all equipment, materials, and supplies used in the production of finished products.

As a PEZA-registered enterprise, BBSON is entitled to income tax holidays and incentives provided by the Philippine government. Our rapid growth is driven by well-trained personnel and a commitment to delivering consistent quality to every client.

What We Do

Specialized coating services for industrial and electronic product manufacturers, focused on surface quality and process consistency.

Metal Spray Painting

Powder coat and spray paint application on metal components using Korean-manufactured precision equipment to ensure uniform coating coverage.

Plastic Spray Painting

Surface coating for plastic parts and sub-components used in electronics and related assemblies, with attention to adhesion and finish quality.

Component Fabrication

Supporting fabrication operations including drilling and tapping for metal and electronic sub-components, integrated within the coating workflow.
